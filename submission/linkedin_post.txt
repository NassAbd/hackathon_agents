👻 Built GHOST at Agents, Everywhere — the global hackathon by OpenAI × AI Tinkerers.

Every app understands what happens inside it. GHOST understands why you move between them.

As you move across your workflow, GHOST follows the context you encounter, builds a live graph of entities and relationships, and progressively infers what you're trying to accomplish.

When you're ready to act, that context is already there — grounded, connected, and ready to use.

No prompt required.

The prompt isn't what you type.
The prompt is what you do.

Built with OpenAI Agents SDK, Next.js, React Flow — and a lot of iteration in a few hours.
